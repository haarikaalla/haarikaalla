### 🔭 Research Interests & Frontier Topics

<p>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Agent%20Orchestration-6C63FF?style=flat-square"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Vision--Language%20Models-6C63FF?style=flat-square"/>

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/RLHF%20%2F%20RLAIF-6C63FF?style=flat-square"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Mechanistic%20Interpretability-6C63FF?style=flat-square"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Physics--Informed%20GNNs-6C63FF?style=flat-square"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Test--Time%20Reasoning-6C63FF?style=flat-square"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/World%20Models-6C63FF?style=flat-square"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Neurosymbolic%20AI-6C63FF?style=flat-square"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Causal%20Inference-6C63FF?style=flat-square"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Federated%20Learning-6C63FF?style=flat-square"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Model%20Compression-6C63FF?style=flat-square"/>
</p>

- **Agent orchestration** — coordinating multiple specialist LLM agents (planner, retriever, critic, executor) into a single reliable workflow instead of one monolithic prompt
- **Vision-language & embodied models (VLMs/VLAs)** — grounding language in visual and spatial input for perception-driven agents and robotics
- **Mixture-of-Experts & sparse architectures** — scaling model capacity without scaling inference cost by routing tokens through specialized sub-networks
- **RLHF / RLAIF & preference optimization** — aligning model behavior with human or AI-generated feedback signals beyond next-token prediction
- **Mechanistic interpretability** — reverse-engineering what's actually happening inside a model, not just measuring output quality
- **Test-time reasoning & inference-time compute scaling** — models that "think longer" via search, self-critique, or chain-of-thought rather than relying purely on parameter scale
- **World models** — learned simulators that let agents plan by predicting outcomes before acting, central to robotics and autonomous systems
- **Neurosymbolic AI** — combining neural pattern-matching with symbolic logic for tasks that need verifiable, structured reasoning
- **Causal inference in ML systems** — moving models from correlation to counterfactual, intervention-aware reasoning
- **Federated & privacy-preserving learning** — training across distributed data without centralizing sensitive information
- **Model compression & efficient inference** — quantization, distillation, and pruning to make large models deployable at the edge
- **Physics-informed graph neural networks** — embedding domain physics into GNNs to catch cascading failures (climate, infrastructure) before they happen
